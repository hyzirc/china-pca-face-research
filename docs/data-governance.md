# Data Governance and Privacy

## Public Repository Rules

Do not upload:
- Original identifiable face photographs.
- Unauthorized or restricted datasets.
- Personal biometric information.
- Private keys, access tokens or passwords.
- Unreviewed derived facial images or face embeddings.

## Dataset Registration

For each dataset, record:
- Source and version.
- Collection period and geographic scope.
- License and terms of use.
- Consent or other applicable legal basis.
- Permitted research uses.
- Whether redistribution is allowed.
- Download date and SHA-256 checksum.

## Storage

Raw and processed datasets remain in local storage
unless redistribution is explicitly permitted.

The .gitignore rules are safeguards, not encryption.
Always inspect staged files before committing.

## Derived Results

Before publishing derived face images, embeddings or
other outputs, review dataset terms and privacy risks.
